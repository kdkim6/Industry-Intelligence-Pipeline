Evidence-Driven Porter's Five Forces Scorer

Turns a structured industry brief into whole-number force scores that can be defended with traceable evidence. Scale: 5 = strong force / heavy pressure on industry profits; 1 = weak force.

Method

1. Load evidence
Paste a structured JSON brief or edit the preloaded Chipotle evidence.
2. Rate each fact
Impact is 1-5 for force strength; weight is 1-3 for strategic importance.
3. Calculate
Weighted average is rounded to a whole number, as required.
4. Citation gate
Every force must have at least three nonblank citations or the tool flags it.

Important: This assignment's direction is the opposite of the older Neighbor spreadsheet example. Here, a higher force score means a less attractive industry.

Structured industry brief input

The default data below is the Chipotle / Mexican Restaurants in the US analysis. It combines the September 19 industry brief with Chipotle's 2025 Form 10-K.

Submission PDF

This tool can generate the assignment PDF directly from the current scores and evidence. The PDF includes the working tool link, all five force scores with rationales and cited facts, the overall industry-attractiveness read, the least-confident force, and the required reflection.
